The by-design e2e suite now covers Reliability (R2, R5): a genuine dropped-connection transport failure from the dot-ai stub degrades to the same stable 502 envelope as an HTTP-level upstream error, and an Ask completed with no Grafana datasource configured renders a non-fatal Current (no error, a real answer) while the per-datasource "missing" notes — dropped from the rendered Current once `rewriteCurrent` replaces it with a summary — are confirmed on the one surface they actually survive: the POST body Playwright observes leaving the browser.
